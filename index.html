About the Event

What is SEMICOLON?

SEMICOLON is an Inter-School Ideathon hosted by TechTrack under Birla Global University — the BGU Tech Club. It's a space where student innovators come together to ideate, design, and pitch solutions to real world technical challenges.

This Year's Theme

Campus & EdTech Solutions

In programming, a semicolon signals that the statement doesn't stop there — there's more to write. That's our philosophy. We challenge you to think beyond the obvious: what tools, systems, or experiences could transform how students learn, connect, and thrive on campus?

Smart Campus Learning Management Student Welfare Tech Digital Assessment Campus Automation Peer Learning Platforms Academic Analytics

Eligibility

Who Can Participate?

SEMICOLON is open to all college students who dare to think differently. Build your dream team and bring your boldest ideas.

🎓

Inter-School Students

Open to any undergraduate or postgraduate college student. Cross-college teams are encouraged — bring together your best minds.

👥

Team Size: 1–2 Members

Compete solo or in a team of up to 2. Cross-disciplinary teams from different colleges are allowed and welcomed.

🆓

Completely Free

Zero registration fee. Just register your team and show up with your best ideas. All you need is passion and creativity.

🌐

All Disciplines Welcome

You don't need to be a coder. Ideas from design, management, social science, and any field are equally valued.
